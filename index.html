About

The working bio.

J'myle writes the kind of material that earns the laugh on the second beat, then steals an extra one on the third.

The short version: Salt Lake kid, four years on a Navy minesweeper, a decade on Northwest stages out of Seattle, now back home. The long version is an hour of stand-up called Twink or Swim.

He has studied under Alice Fraser, Tiff Stevenson, and Andrew Frank.
