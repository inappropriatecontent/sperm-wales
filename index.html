About

The working bio.

J'myle writes the kind of material that earns the laugh on the second beat, then steals an extra one on the third. Autobiographical without being indulgent. Clever without being cold.

The short version: Salt Lake kid, four years in the Navy on a wooden minesweeper in the Western Pacific, a decade working Northwest stages out of Seattle, now back home in Salt Lake. The long version is an hour of stand-up called Twink or Swim.

He's hosted and featured at Emerald City and Tacoma Comedy Clubs, has performed twice at The Hollywood Comedy Store, and has studied under Alice Fraser, Tiff Stevenson, and Andrew Frank.
